How placebos work

Research, books & talks

A starting library on the science and stories behind the placebo effect. These are third-party sources; the views expressed are those of their authors.

Published studies

  • Harvard Health: The power of the placebo effect — how belief and expectation can influence healing and, in some cases, match conventional treatment.
  • National Library of Medicine: the case for more placebo research — a call to build better theory around the placebo effect.
  • Nature (Scientific Reports): placebos and emotion — tests whether placebo-induced expectations can improve emotion regulation.
  • National Library of Medicine: placebos in animals — pharmacological evidence that placebo responses occur in animals as well as humans.
  • Harvard Health blog: the placebo effect, amazing and real — measurable physiological changes seen in placebo responders.
  • University of Maryland: research on the placebo effect — what is known, and what remains to be studied, about placebo mechanisms.

Articles

  • Better Health Channel (Australia): the placebo effect — a plain-language overview, including the origin of the word “placebo.”
  • Medical News Today: how the placebo effect works — an explainer on the psychology behind placebo responses.
  • Verywell Mind: the placebo effect — an accessible introduction to fake treatments and real responses.
  • Time: the placebo effect and pain — reporting on studies of placebos and pain relief.

Books & resources

  • Time: people taking placebo pills for health problems — coverage of Dr. Ted Kaptchuk’s work on placebos taken openly.
  • The Improvised Life: self-administered placebos — a reflection on Kevin Kelly’s comments about placebos.
  • Samueli Foundation placebo guide (PDF) — a clinical guide by Dr. Wayne Jonas on expectation, ritual, and the “meaning response.”
  • The Power of Placebos, by Jeremy Howick — an interdisciplinary look at placebos and nocebos.
  • How Your Mind Can Heal Your Body, by Dr. David R. Hamilton — how thoughts and emotions can shape the body.
  • You Are the Placebo, by Joe Dispenza — neuroscience, behavior, and belief in the placebo effect.

Videos & talks

Search for these experts to watch or listen: Dr. Ted Kaptchuk (Harvard Program in Placebo Studies), Dr. David R. Hamilton, Erik Vance (Suggestible You), and Dr. Andrew Huberman’s episode on placebo, belief, and mindset.
